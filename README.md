**Midnight Vault** — an original top-down stealth heist game, built for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty.

You are the thief, working three floors — the First National Bank, the Museum of Glass, and the Meridian Vault. Each floor is one screen of connected rooms: patrol the gaps in the guards' vision cones, read the cameras' sweeps, cross on the dark rugs and behind desks where detection crawls, lift the gem from the vault, pocket every cash bag you dare, and reach the getaway van before the alarm meter does its work. A full meter is not an instant arrest: break every line of sight and it drains, so a blown moment can still be salvaged — if you planned an exit from the light.

Pure vanilla JavaScript and canvas, written from scratch for this bounty: no engine, no libraries, no assets, no network. Sound is a handful of WebAudio blips synthesised in code. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to tiptoe, P pauses, M mutes.

To play it locally: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. To hand it in: `node main.ts` copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to `/output` and writes the dossier.
